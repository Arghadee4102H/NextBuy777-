Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27 October 2026

Welcome to NextBuy 77. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you visit our website or purchase products from us.

By using our website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will obtain it before processing your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or shop on our website, we may collect the following types of information, depending on the features you use:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Billing and shipping address

Order Information

  • Products purchased
  • Order details and transaction references
  • Delivery and order status

Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. We may receive payment confirmation, transaction references, and payment status. We do not intend to store complete payment card details on our own systems.

Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage data, where supported by our website and its services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process and fulfil orders.
  • Arrange shipping and delivery.
  • Send order confirmations and delivery updates.
  • Respond to customer enquiries.
  • Process payments and address payment-related issues.
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized activity.
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting, tax, and regulatory obligations.
  • Send promotional communications where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.

3. Payment Security

NextBuy 77 may use third-party payment gateways to process online transactions.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security practices. Customers should review the relevant provider's terms when making a payment.

We take reasonable steps to protect the transaction information available to us. However, no online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell customers' personal information to third parties.

We may share relevant information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our business, including:

  • Courier and delivery partners: To deliver your orders.
  • Payment providers: To process transactions and verify payments.
  • Website hosting and technology providers: To maintain and operate our website.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: Where reasonably necessary to meet legal obligations or protect our legal rights.

We aim to share only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose, subject to applicable law.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or third-party services may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, improve performance, and understand website usage.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

Where required by law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order fulfilment, customer support, legal compliance, and dispute resolution, subject to applicable retention requirements.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permitted.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise a concern or grievance regarding the handling of your information.

To exercise an applicable right, please contact us using the contact details provided on our website. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

8. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use external services, including payment gateways and courier tracking systems.

We do not control the privacy practices of independent third parties. Their services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

We encourage customers to review those policies before providing personal information directly to such services.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed at children. Individuals who are not legally eligible to enter into a binding agreement should use our website with the involvement of a parent or legal guardian, as applicable.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by law. If you believe personal information has been collected unlawfully, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our business operations, website features, service providers, or applicable laws.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date. Material changes will be communicated where required by law.

11. Contact and Privacy Grievances

If you have questions, requests, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us through the official contact details displayed on the NextBuy 77 website.

We will review and respond to privacy-related concerns in accordance with applicable law.
