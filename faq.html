Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Buy Next. Buy Better.

Welcome to NextBuy 77! We've answered some common questions to make your shopping experience easier. If you need further assistance, please contact us through the contact details available on our website.

1. What is NextBuy 77?

NextBuy 77 is an online shopping store offering fashion accessories, imitation jewellery, bracelets, earrings, perfumes, and other lifestyle products. Our goal is to provide a convenient shopping experience with our motto: Buy Next. Buy Better.

2. How can I place an order?

Browse our website, select your favourite products, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery details, choose an available payment method, and complete your order.

3. Do I need an account to place an order?

If guest checkout is available, you can place an order without creating an account. If our website requires an account, please follow the registration or login instructions during checkout.

4. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept the payment methods displayed at checkout. Available options include direct Manual UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and other supported digital payment services.

5. How will I know if my order has been confirmed?

After placing your order, check for an order confirmation message on the website or through the contact method you provided, if confirmation notifications are enabled.

6. How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location, product availability, and courier service. Please check the estimated delivery time displayed on the product page or during checkout, where available.

7. How can I track my order?

If tracking is available for your order, we will share the tracking details or courier information through the communication method provided during checkout.

8. Do you charge shipping fees?

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before you complete your purchase.

9. Can I cancel my order after placing it?

NextBuy 77 does not currently have a separate Cancellation Policy page. If you need to request a cancellation, please contact us as soon as possible with your order details. Requests will be reviewed according to the order status and applicable law.

10. Can I return a product or request a refund?

NextBuy 77 does not currently have a separate Returns and Refunds Policy page. If you receive a damaged, defective, incorrect, or materially misdescribed product, please contact us with your order details and relevant photographs, where available. We will review the issue in accordance with applicable law.

11. Are the products shown on the website exactly as pictured?

We make reasonable efforts to display accurate product images and descriptions. However, slight differences in colour, appearance, or packaging may occur due to lighting, screen settings, or product variations.

12. Are your jewellery products made of real gold or silver?

Our imitation jewellery products should not be assumed to be made of genuine gold or silver unless the specific product description explicitly states and supports that claim. Please review each product's description and material information before ordering.

13. How should I use and store perfumes?

Follow the instructions and safety information provided with the product. Store perfumes in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat, and review the ingredient information where available.

14. Is my personal information safe?

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we handle. Customer information may be shared with payment providers, delivery partners, and other service providers when necessary to process orders and operate our website. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.

15. Do you offer discounts or promotional codes?

Discounts and promotional codes may be available during selected promotions. Any applicable eligibility requirements, expiry dates, and usage conditions will be communicated with the offer.

16. What should I do if I receive the wrong product?

Please contact us promptly and provide your order number, a description of the issue, and photographs where available. We will review your concern and explain the next steps in accordance with applicable law.

17. How can I contact NextBuy 77?

For questions about products, orders, delivery, or other concerns, please use the official contact details displayed on our website.

Thank you for shopping with NextBuy 77!
